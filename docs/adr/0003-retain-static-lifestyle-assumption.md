# Retain the static lifestyle assumption

The study represents each patient's questionnaire responses as time-invariant values repeated across source years. Collection timing has not been established, and the author confirms that this is a research assumption rather than verified evidence that responses were available at every prediction occasion. Retain this treatment to preserve the published experimental framing, and document its limitations in the public methods description.

Temporal checks can verify that annual clinical measurements, rolling histories, and cumulative glucose predictors use no years later than the source year. They cannot establish chronological availability of undated questionnaire responses. Documentation must distinguish these checks from the unresolved timing of lifestyle measurements; calling the fields static must not imply a verified baseline collection date.

Replacing the static covariates with dated observations, excluding them, or adding a clinical-only sensitivity experiment would change or extend the research protocol and is outside this refactor's agreed scope.
